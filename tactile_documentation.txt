Crustle Wall

-        The goal of this deck is simple, we use crustles ability to avoid all damage from ex pokemon whilst dealing damage over time with crustle, kanghaskhan and ogrepon

-        Ogrepon serves as a secondary wall against decks that don’t play pokemon ex, ogrepons ability mitigates all damage from pokemon with an ability, providing good coverage against all pokemon.

-        Overall the decks game plan is to wall against all attacks with ogrepon and crustle whilst chipping away at our opponents pokemon with crustle and ogrepons attacks

-        Kanghaskan is here for big damage when we need it and as an early game draw engine, to see as many cards as possible.

 

-        Early game we want to lead with kanghaskhan to draw cards and eventually set up into our crustles (and ogrepon if we need them)

 

-        Buddy buddy poffins helps us set up our dwebbles so that we can later evolve them into our crustless later

 

-        Jumbo ice cream is useful to heal kanghaskhan and ogrepon off from big hits if they take them, but also can be used to heal crustle if the opponents opts to use single prize attackers to get around crustles ability

 

-        Pokegear helps us find supporters when we need them and switch is to help switch since our pokemon do have somewhat high retreat costs ultra ball helps us find evolutions or any pokemon when we need them.

 

-        Community center helps us heal off some chip damage and team rockets factory synergises with team rockets petrel, which when used in synergy allow us to find any item card we want and draw two cards

 

-        Bosses orders is used to bring up weak pokemon that are hiding on the bench and take them out for more prize cards and can also be used as disruption to push a pokemon that they want in the active to the bench

 

-        Colress tenacity helps us get our stadiums and a special energy card (which we play a lot of) and Hilda does the same thing but instead of a stadium we can find a crustle

 

-        Lillies determination is our go to draw supporter as it allows to see 6-8 new cards in a turn helping us find what we need to start walling

 

-        Morty’s convitiction is a secondary draw supporter which late game can help us see more cards and find whatever we might need

 

-        Team rockets petrel can be used to find any trainer card and subsequently any pokemon through ultra ball and poffin, or a supporter card for next turn

 

-        Xerosics machinations is a disrupt supporter best used when the opponents hand is full to slow them down and take control of tempo

 

-        And heros cape is best used when you know that a pokemon would be knocked out by an attack from the opponents pokemon the following turn but would survive with an extra 50 hp

 

-        Aside from the grass energy we play the growing grass energy to add extra hp and mist energy to stop special conditions, the rocky fightin energy is for ogrepon because it needs fighting energy to attack and the spiky energy is to get a bit of extra damage off
